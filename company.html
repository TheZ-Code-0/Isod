Genesis & Evolution

From Idea to Economic Sovereignty

Our journey began in 2017 in Bekasi City as a youth movement dedicated to inclusivity studies, establishing the Indonesian School of Diversity (ISOD). We built an epistemic community exploring how diversity serves as the foundation for a stable social order, practicing inclusivity as an operational standard.

The year 2025 marks our grand transformation. Raga ISOD Nusantara channels these inclusive values into productive economic sectors. By connecting local farmers from diverse backgrounds into a unified production ecosystem, we believe equitable economic prosperity strengthens social cohesion across Indonesia.

Global Vision

An Ethical Supply Chain for the World

We bring Nusantara's premium commodities to the global market with the highest standards of integrity. Beyond commodities, we offer an "Ethical Supply Chain"—inviting international business partners to collaborate under the principles of equality, transparency, and democratically responsible governance.

"We guarantee that every harvest we distribute carries a story of justice for local farming communities and harmony in diversity."

Why Choose Us?

Driven by Value, Backed by Knowledge

Guaranteed Supply Stability

Direct, inclusive partnerships with our network of local farmers ensure a secure and consistent volume of commodities, eliminating the risk of delivery failures caused by seasonal market disruptions.

Full Traceability & Global Compliance

Our products feature end-to-end traceability from farm to shipment. This guarantees your products are 100% compliant with international laws, environmental regulations (such as anti-deforestation laws), and labor standards.

Cost Efficiency Through Direct Sourcing

By bypassing traditional brokers and middlemen, our direct relationship with farmers creates a clean and lean cost structure. This enables us to offer premium-grade products at highly competitive export prices.

One-Stop Diverse Sourcing

We aggregate Indonesia’s vast agricultural diversity under a single, professional management team. You save time and logistics management costs by eliminating the need to source from multiple vendors for different commodities.

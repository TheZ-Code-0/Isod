Our Story

From the Tree of Life

Nuri Coconutnesia emerged from the lush tropical landscapes of Indonesia, where the coconut tree is revered as the "tree of life." For generations, Indonesian communities have harnessed the versatility of coconuts.

Our mission is to share this incredible natural resource with the world while supporting sustainable farming practices and preserving the traditional knowledge of our coconut farming communities.
